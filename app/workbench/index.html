Overview

Welcome, this is your console for building and shipping client websites with Stardrive. It goes in two steps: design a template, then build a site from it for each client. Everything you make is private to your account.

Agency perk. Queue a whole set of client sites at once, submit them together, and come back later, everything designed, copy-written, and ready to publish. Each build answers the same questions a site built one at a time does, so what comes back is finished, not a shell. Runs overnight on the model provider's Batch API at roughly half the cost of building one at a time; we email you when a batch is done.

Your hosting, your accounts, your site. Connect your own Vercel, Turso, and GitHub credentials and every deploy goes to infrastructure you own, on whatever plan tier you choose, Stardrive never owns your website. Set them once and every client site you build publishes here automatically, no re-entering, ideal when you host for your clients. Only override on a site when that client hosts it themselves. Tokens are encrypted at rest, never shown again after saving, and only ever receive the assembled site: no part of the Stardrive engine is ever pushed to your accounts.

One click to a live URL. Where your sites go live; set the token once and every build publishes here. Free Hobby tier works; upgrade on your own terms.
