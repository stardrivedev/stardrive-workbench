Overview

The d4 site engine as a service: map your intake questionnaire, bring your own templates, assemble real Next.js sites, and deploy them to hosting you own. Your API key is your license; everything you upload stays private to your account.

Get started

  1. Mint a key (operator machine, once): 
    node scripts/make-key.mjs --name "my key"
  2. Paste it in the API-key box above and press Save. Every panel here uses it; so can your own scripts.
  3. Make a template. Use the Template Studio to generate one by chatting with a model, or upload a folder you built against the Rulebook on the Templates page.
  4. Assemble a site from any template — see the API Reference for the two-call flow.

How privacy works here

        
Three shelves of templates. The bundled d4 catalog is shared with every account. Your imports — uploaded here, generated in the Studio, or ported from your own studio tooling — belong to your account alone: no other licensee can list, read, build from, or collide with them. Keys minted with the same --account share one shelf, so your CI key and your dashboard key see the same library.
